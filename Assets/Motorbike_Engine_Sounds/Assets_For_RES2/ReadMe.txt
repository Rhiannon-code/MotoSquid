If you want to use this sound pack with "Realistic Engine Sounds 2" unity asset,
import "RES2_Motorbike_Prefabs.unitypackage" into your project to get the prefabs and demo scenes made for Realistic Engine Sounds 2.
After importing the above mentioned .unitypackage, the new engine sound prefabs will be added in 3 different quality levels into the following folder: Motorbike_Engine_Sounds\Assets\Prefabs\Engine_Prefabs
6 demo scenes will be available in the following folder: Motorbike_Engine_Sounds\DemoScene
Drag and drop one of the newly imported prefab into a vehicle physics compatibility prefab and you're ready to test these new sounds (Realistic Engine Sounds 2 comes with pre-made compatibility made for multiple vehicle physic controllers).
Demo scenes with names "RequiresBikeController_MotorbikeDemo" are made for testing these engine sounds with your vehicle physics controller. Buttons for switching engine sounds and RES2 prefabs are ready to be used with your vehicle, follow the scene's tutorial from the Documentation about how to setup to test all Motorbike engine sounds in this scene.

Realistic Engine Sounds 2 available here: https://bit.ly/3aZ50Ag

Importing the unitypackage without Realistic Engine Sounds 2 already being in your project, will produce errors in the newly imported prefabs.
